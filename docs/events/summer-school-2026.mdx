# Intensive Ontology Summer School

## PHI 605SEM-SMI (13336): The World's Ontology Ecosystem

**August 3–7, 2026**  
**Washington, DC and Online**

An intensive hybrid graduate seminar offered by the University at Buffalo and taught by John Beverley and Barry Smith. The course introduces ontology as both a philosophical discipline and a practical technology for modern information systems, with special focus on Basic Formal Ontology (BFO), ontology engineering, knowledge graphs, and applications in science, industry, government, and AI.

## Course Overview

This summer school is designed for graduate students, researchers, government professionals, industry practitioners, and others interested in applied ontology, semantic technologies, knowledge representation, data integration, and AI.

Participants may join in person in Washington, DC, or online. The course may be taken for UB credit by eligible students. Non-credit online participation is also available.

## Topics Covered

- Basic Formal Ontology (BFO)
- BFO and ISO/IEC 21838
- Ontology engineering tradecraft
- Knowledge graphs and semantic interoperability
- National security, finance, law, and scientific applications
- AI-driven ontology construction systems

## Course Format

**Workshop Week: August 3–7, 2026**

Lectures, hands-on workshops, group discussions, and debates.

**Post-Workshop Component**

Zoom sessions, ontology exercises, a final presentation, and a seminar paper for students seeking credit.
