---
title: Credentialing & Certification
---

NCOR provides independent, nonprofit credentialing services for ontologies and ontology engineering practices, grounded in formal ontology and international standards.

Our credentialing activities focus on:
- Conformance to Basic Formal Ontology (BFO), standardized as ISO/IEC 21838-2
- Alignment with the Common Core Ontologies (CCO)
- Evaluation of ontology engineering best practices beyond any single implementation

NCOR’s credentialing role is independent of commercial vendors, government agencies, and implementers. Certifications are issued based on scientific rigor, formal review, and documented evidence.
