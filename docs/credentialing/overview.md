title: Credentialing & Certification
---

NCOR provides **independent, nonprofit certification** services for ontology conformance and ontology engineering best practices, grounded in formal ontology and internationally recognized standards.

Our certifications are designed for organizations adopting BFO- and CCO-aligned semantic technologies, including enterprises, government and defense organizations, AI and data governance teams, and standards and audit stakeholders.

> **Note:** NCOR is not an ISO certification body. We provide independent certification and registry services informed by relevant standards, including ISO/IEC 21838-2 (BFO).
