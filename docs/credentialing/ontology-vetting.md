---
title: Ontology Vetting & Certification
---

NCOR operates an independent ontology vetting and certification program grounded in formal ontology and international standards.

The process includes:
1. Ontology submission with documented scope, classes, and relations
2. Automated structural validation (e.g., OWL2 conformance checks)
3. Expert review by a three-member NCOR panel
4. Remediation and resubmission, if needed
5. Certification and registry entry (valid for 12 months)
6. Annual renewal or continuous audit

Specialty certifications include:
- Alignment with ISO/IEC 21838-2 (Basic Formal Ontology)
- Alignment with the Common Core Ontologies (CCO)

Each certification includes a documented audit trail and registry identifier.
