NCOR is a nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing formal ontology, open standards, and independent credentialing in support of trustworthy AI, data governance, and scientific interoperability.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, NCOR relies on charitable contributions to sustain its public mission and community-driven work.

## Our Mission

Donations to the National Center for Ontological Research (NCOR) support:

- Independent stewardship of open ontology standards
- Scientific rigor in ontology evaluation and certification
- Open educational resources and community training
- Long-term sustainability of formal ontology infrastructure

NCOR operates independently of commercial vendors and technology implementers. Contributions directly support open science and public-interest work.

## What Donations Support

Your support helps fund:

- **Open educational resources**  
  Public documentation, tutorials, and training materials for ontology and semantic technologies.

- **Student fellowships and early-career support**  
  Opportunities for students and early-career researchers to engage in applied ontology work.

- **Community workshops and meetings**  
  Open events, working groups, and forums that bring together academia, industry, and government.

- **Stewardship of open standards**  
  Ongoing maintenance, governance participation, and evolution of foundational ontology standards.

## What Donors Can Expect

NCOR donors receive:

- Periodic updates on NCOR activities and initiatives
- Invitations to NCOR-sponsored meetings and working groups
- Visibility into how contributions support open standards and community efforts

NCOR does not provide commercial endorsements or preferential treatment in credentialing decisions.
